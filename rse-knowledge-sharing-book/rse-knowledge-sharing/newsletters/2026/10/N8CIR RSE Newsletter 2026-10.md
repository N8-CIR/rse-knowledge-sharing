## Events

### (External) Edinburgh RIE Community and Co-design Day 2026

**Date**: 10am-4pm 11th November 2026, registrations and refreshments from 9.30am

The research infrastructure engineer (RIE) community and co-design days bring together RIEs from across a region and beyond to share knowledge and experience, build networks, and contribute to the ACIT Hub's training plans.

The day will combine short, scene-setting talks to guide the day's discussions and structured interactive sessions where participants will explore and develop RIE training ideas.
This RIE community and co-design day will build on the discussions and priorities identified in previous ACIT Hub co-design events, where research infrastructure engineers (RIEs) highlighted key training topics and preferred formats (e-learning, hackathons, webinars, in person courses). The focus of the co-design part of the day is to move from “what we need” to “how we design it”; developing concrete training plans that ACIT Hub can take forward into course and event development.

For more details and registration please visit:
  [https://acit.surrey.ac.uk/events/2026-11-11_CodesignEdinburgh.html](https://acit.surrey.ac.uk/events/2026-11-11_CodesignEdinburgh.html)

## News

### N8CIR at the Leeds Digital Festival

What a turnout and buzz of the sharing of ideas and the building of future Academia + Industry collaborations! This event was a collaboration between N8CIR and University of Leeds ARC team to bring together academics, RSEs and industry professionals to discuss how we can collaborate and knowledge share in the future.
We had attendees representing academia and industry from student to expert and a poster and talk offer to match. A relaxed networking session before and after the presentations revealed many collaboration opportunities from trusted research environments to job transitions.
Read our [blog post](https://n8-cir.github.io/rse-knowledge-sharing/rse-knowledge-sharing/blog-posts/2026/10/Digital%20Research%20Futures%20Showcasing%20Talent%2C%20Technology%20and%20Research%20Impact.html) on the event for a more in depth review and our thoughts for next steps.

### N8CIR Internships 2026

The N8CIR Summer Internships 2026 finished up with a fantastic showcase of diverse talent from this years cohort of interns. Each intern had the opportunity to present their work in an 8 minute presentation followed by a breakout session to discuss their work with other interns and supervisors. We received a lot of positive feedback from interns, academic and technical supervisors and have taken on suggestions to make next year even better (see above for details).

### Newcastle University - Head of Research Computing

**Deadline: 23/10/26**
[Head of Research Computing Job Details | Newcastle University](https://jobs.ncl.ac.uk/job/Newcastle-Head-of-Research-Computing/1440880533/)
We are seeking an experienced and collaborative Head of Research Computing to lead the development, delivery and continuous improvement of research computing services across Newcastle University. This is an exciting opportunity to shape a strategically important service that enables world-class research, supports innovation and helps researchers make effective use of advanced computing, cloud, software and data capabilities. Working closely with the Director of IT Service Delivery and Research Computing, academic colleagues, research teams and wider IT specialists, you will provide strategic and operational leadership for research computing. You will ensure that services are secure, reliable, scalable and aligned to the evolving needs of a diverse research community.
